### Promotional Text (170 char max)
MasjidFeed keeps you connected to your masjid's daily rhythm, wherever you are. Requires masjid participation.

**Prayer Times**
See Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha with Athan and Iqama times, a live countdown to the next prayer, and Jumuah session details. Set custom reminders per prayer.

**Home Screen Widgets**
Add a widget to your home screen for the next prayer time and countdown, or see a full day's schedule at a glance, all without opening the app.

**Events & Announcements**
Browse upcoming classes, fundraisers, and community events with full details and images. Add events straight to your calendar, including recurring series. Follow a live feed of masjid announcements and news.

**Qibla Compass**
Find the Qibla direction instantly using your device's compass and location.

**Community & Giving**
Get contact info, directions, and a quick way to call or email your masjid. Support your masjid with a direct link to its donation page. MasjidFeed never stores or processes your payment details.
